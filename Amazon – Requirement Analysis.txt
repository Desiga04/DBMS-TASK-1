 Amazon – Requirement Analysis

 Introduction

Amazon is a popular e-commerce platform that allows customers to purchase a wide variety of products online. Users can create an account, search for products, compare prices, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries. Amazon provides a convenient and user-friendly shopping experience for customers and also provides sellers with a platform to manage and sell their products.

 Functional Requirements

The functional requirements describe the main activities that Amazon should perform. The system should allow users to **register and log in** to their accounts. Customers should be able to **search and browse products**, view product details, prices, images, ratings, and reviews. Users should be able to **add or remove products from the shopping cart**, place orders, and make payments using different payment methods. The system should provide **order tracking, cancellation, and return options**. Customers should also be able to give ratings and reviews for products. Sellers should be able to add and manage their products and orders.

 Non-Functional Requirements

The non-functional requirements describe the quality and performance of the Amazon system. The system should provide **high security** to protect customer information and payment details. It should have **good performance** and respond quickly to user requests. Amazon should be **reliable and available 24/7** with minimum downtime. The system should be **scalable** so that it can support millions of users and large numbers of products and orders. It should also be **easy to use, maintain, and update** and should work properly on different devices and web browsers.
